# UK Science Curriculum — Grades 5 to 10
### Aligned with UK National Curriculum (KS2–KS4)

---

## Grade 5 (Year 6)

**Biology**
- Living things: classification of plants, animals, microorganisms
- Animals including humans: heart, blood, circulatory system
- Plants: nutrition, water transport, reproduction

**Physics**
- Light: reflection, shadows, how we see
- Forces: gravity, air resistance, water resistance, levers, pulleys

**Chemistry**
- Properties of everyday materials
- Changes of state: melting, boiling, evaporation

---

## Grade 6 (Year 7)

**Biology**
- Cells: animal and plant cell structure
- Reproduction in plants and animals
- Ecosystems: food chains, interdependence, adaptation

**Chemistry**
- Particle model: solids, liquids, gases
- Pure substances and mixtures
- Elements, compounds, and the periodic table (introduction)

**Physics**
- Forces: speed, distance, time calculations
- Sound: vibrations, pitch, volume, echoes
- Energy: stores and transfers, renewable vs non-renewable

---

## Grade 7 (Year 8)

**Biology**
- Nutrition and digestion: enzymes and the digestive system
- Breathing and gas exchange: lungs and respiration
- Skeleton and muscles: movement and support

**Chemistry**
- Atoms and elements: atomic structure (basic)
- Chemical reactions: reactants, products, word equations
- Acids and alkalis: pH scale, indicators, neutralisation

**Physics**
- Electricity: current, voltage, series and parallel circuits
- Magnetism: magnets, magnetic fields, electromagnets
- Space: solar system, seasons, day and night

---

## Grade 8 (Year 9)

**Biology**
- Genetics and evolution: DNA, inherited traits, natural selection
- Photosynthesis: equation, conditions, leaf structure
- Respiration: aerobic and anaerobic

**Chemistry**
- Periodic table: groups, periods, properties of metals/non-metals
- Reactions of metals: reactivity series, displacement reactions
- Earth's resources: rock cycle, carbon cycle, climate change

**Physics**
- Motion: distance-time graphs, speed, velocity, acceleration
- Pressure: in solids, liquids, and gases
- Waves: properties of transverse and longitudinal waves

---

## Grade 9 (Year 10 — GCSE Start)

**Biology**
- Cell biology: mitosis, specialised cells, diffusion, osmosis
- Organisation: organs and systems, enzymes
- Infection and response: pathogens, immune system, vaccines
- Bioenergetics: photosynthesis and respiration equations

**Chemistry**
- Atomic structure and the periodic table
- Bonding, structure, and properties of matter
- Quantitative chemistry: moles, reactions, concentration
- Chemical changes: electrolysis, acids and bases

**Physics**
- Energy: efficiency, power, specific heat capacity
- Electricity: resistance, power, domestic electricity
- Particle model of matter: density, changes of state
- Atomic structure: radioactivity, nuclear decay

---

## Grade 10 (Year 11 — GCSE Completion)

**Biology**
- Genetics and evolution: inheritance, Punnett squares, evolution
- Ecology: food webs, biodiversity, human impact on ecosystems
- Homeostasis: nervous system, hormones, diabetes

**Chemistry**
- Rates of reaction and energy changes
- Fuels and Earth's atmosphere: greenhouse gases, global warming
- Using resources: water treatment, life cycle assessment

**Physics**
- Forces: Newton's Laws, stopping distances, momentum
- Waves: electromagnetic spectrum, uses and dangers
- Magnetism and electromagnetism: motors, generators
- Space physics: stellar evolution, Big Bang theory
