# US Science Curriculum — Grades 5 to 10
### Aligned with Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)

---

## Grade 5

**Life Science**
- Ecosystems: food chains, food webs, energy flow
- Plants and animals: growth, reproduction, survival
- Human body: organ systems overview

**Physical Science**
- Matter: properties of solids, liquids, and gases
- Energy: heat, light, sound, and electricity basics
- Forces and motion: pushes, pulls, gravity

**Earth & Space Science**
- Earth's layers: crust, mantle, core
- Weather and climate: patterns and changes
- Solar system: planets, moon phases, stars

---

## Grade 6

**Life Science**
- Cells: structure and function (plant vs animal cells)
- Microorganisms: bacteria, viruses, fungi
- Ecosystems: biotic and abiotic factors

**Physical Science**
- Atoms and molecules: introduction to matter
- Energy: forms and transformations
- Simple machines: levers, pulleys, gears

**Earth & Space Science**
- Rock cycle: igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic
- Water cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation
- Earth's atmosphere: layers and composition

---

## Grade 7

**Life Science**
- Cell division: mitosis and growth
- Genetics: traits, heredity, DNA basics
- Human body systems: digestive, respiratory, circulatory

**Physical Science**
- Elements and compounds: periodic table basics
- Chemical vs physical changes
- Density, mass, and volume

**Earth & Space Science**
- Plate tectonics: earthquakes and volcanoes
- Fossils and geological time
- Climate change: causes and effects

---

## Grade 8

**Life Science**
- Evolution: natural selection, adaptation
- Classification of living things (taxonomy)
- Nervous system and senses

**Physical Science**
- Force, motion, and speed (Newton's Laws)
- Waves: sound and light waves
- Electricity and circuits

**Earth & Space Science**
- Astronomy: stars, galaxies, universe
- Earth's history: geological changes over time
- Natural resources and sustainability

---

## Grade 9

**Biology**
- Cell biology: organelles, cell membrane, osmosis
- DNA and genetics: inheritance patterns (Mendelian genetics)
- Photosynthesis and cellular respiration
- Evolution and biodiversity
- Ecology: populations, communities, biomes

**Introduction to Chemistry**
- Atomic structure: protons, neutrons, electrons
- Periodic table: groups and periods
- Chemical bonding: ionic and covalent bonds
- Chemical reactions: balancing equations
- Acids, bases, and pH

---

## Grade 10

**Biology**
- Human physiology: immune system, hormones, reproduction
- Genetic engineering and biotechnology basics
- Environmental science: ecosystems and conservation

**Chemistry**
- States of matter and gas laws
- Solutions: concentration, solubility, mixtures
- Organic chemistry: introduction to carbon compounds
- Reaction rates and energy in reactions

**Physics**
- Motion: speed, velocity, acceleration, graphs
- Forces: gravity, friction, Newton's Laws in depth
- Energy: kinetic, potential, conservation of energy
- Electricity: current, voltage, resistance (Ohm's Law)
