# UAE Science Curriculum — Grades 5 to 10
### Aligned with UAE Ministry of Education (MoE) Standards

---

## Grade 5

**Life Science**
- Living things: characteristics, classification, habitats
- Plant and animal adaptations to desert and marine environments
- Food chains and food webs in UAE ecosystems

**Physical Science**
- Matter: properties of solids, liquids, and gases
- Energy: light, heat, and sound
- Simple machines: levers, wheels, pulleys

**Earth & Space Science**
- Solar system: sun, planets, moons, day and night
- Weather and climate: UAE climate, seasons, wind
- Natural resources: water, oil, minerals in the UAE

---

## Grade 6

**Life Science**
- Cells: basic structure and function
- Human body systems: digestive and circulatory systems
- Ecosystems: biotic and abiotic factors, deserts and seas

**Physical Science**
- Forces: gravity, friction, magnetism
- Electricity: circuits, conductors, insulators, static electricity
- Light: reflection, refraction, colours of light

**Earth & Space Science**
- Rocks and minerals: igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic
- Water cycle: importance of freshwater, desalination in UAE
- Atmosphere: layers, composition, importance

---

## Grade 7

**Biology**
- Cell biology: plant vs animal cells, cell processes
- Reproduction: asexual and sexual reproduction in living things
- Nutrition: food groups, digestion, balanced diet

**Chemistry**
- Matter: atoms, molecules, elements, compounds, mixtures
- States of matter: changes of state, melting, boiling points
- Separation techniques: filtration, distillation, evaporation

**Physics**
- Motion: speed, distance, time
- Forces in depth: balanced and unbalanced forces
- Sound: vibrations, pitch, loudness, wave properties

---

## Grade 8

**Biology**
- Human body: respiratory, excretory, and nervous systems
- Genetics: inherited traits, chromosomes, DNA introduction
- Ecology: food webs, biodiversity, UAE environmental challenges

**Chemistry**
- Elements and the periodic table: groups and periods
- Chemical vs physical changes
- Acids and bases: pH scale, indicators, examples in daily life

**Physics**
- Energy: kinetic and potential energy, conservation
- Heat transfer: conduction, convection, radiation
- Pressure: in liquids and gases, applications

---

## Grade 9

**Biology**
- Cell division: mitosis, growth, and repair
- Photosynthesis and respiration: equations, importance
- Disease and immunity: pathogens, vaccines, antibiotics
- Ecology: carbon and nitrogen cycles, human impact

**Chemistry**
- Atomic structure: Bohr model, electron configuration
- Periodic table: metallic and non-metallic properties, reactions
- Chemical bonding: ionic and covalent compounds
- Chemical reactions: balancing equations, types of reactions

**Physics**
- Kinematics: speed, velocity, acceleration, graphs
- Newton's Laws of Motion: force, mass, acceleration
- Work, energy, and power: calculations and real-life examples
- Waves: sound and light wave properties

---

## Grade 10

**Biology**
- Genetics: Mendelian inheritance, Punnett squares, genetic diseases
- Evolution: natural selection, adaptation, fossil record
- Biotechnology: DNA profiling, genetic modification (introduction)
- Sustainability: environmental protection, UAE Vision 2030 context

**Chemistry**
- Stoichiometry: moles, formula masses, reaction calculations
- Electrochemistry: electrolysis, industrial applications
- Organic chemistry: hydrocarbons, polymers, fuels
- Reaction rates: factors and collision theory

**Physics**
- Electricity: Ohm's Law, circuits, electrical power
- Electromagnetism: magnetic fields, motors, generators
- Radioactivity: types of radiation, half-life, nuclear energy
- Electromagnetic spectrum: uses in medicine, communication
