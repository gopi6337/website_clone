# Singapore Science Curriculum — Grades 5 to 10
### Aligned with Ministry of Education (MOE) Singapore Syllabus

---

## Grade 5 (Primary 5)

**Diversity**
- Diversity of living things: plants, animals, microorganisms
- Diversity of materials: metals, plastics, wood, glass

**Cycles**
- Life cycles of plants and animals
- Water cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation

**Systems**
- Plant system: roots, stems, leaves and their functions
- Human digestive system: organs and processes

**Interactions**
- Food chains and food webs
- Adaptations of plants and animals to their environment

---

## Grade 6 (Primary 6)

**Cycles**
- Reproduction in plants: pollination, seed dispersal
- Reproduction in humans and animals

**Systems**
- Respiratory system: breathing and gas exchange
- Circulatory system: heart, blood vessels, blood

**Interactions**
- Electrical systems: circuits, cells, switches, conductors
- Forces: magnets, gravity, friction

**Energy**
- Forms of energy: light, heat, sound, electrical, kinetic, potential
- Sources of energy: renewable and non-renewable

---

## Grade 7 (Secondary 1)

**Biology**
- Cells: structure of animal and plant cells, cell functions
- Movement of substances: diffusion and osmosis
- Nutrition in humans: balanced diet, digestive enzymes

**Chemistry**
- Measurement and experimental techniques
- Separation techniques: filtration, distillation, chromatography
- Atomic structure: protons, neutrons, electrons

**Physics**
- Physical quantities and measurement
- Forces: types of forces, effects of forces, friction
- Turning effect of forces: moments and levers

---

## Grade 8 (Secondary 2)

**Biology**
- Transport in humans: blood, heart, blood vessels
- Respiration: aerobic and anaerobic respiration equations
- Reproduction in humans: male/female systems, fertilisation

**Chemistry**
- Elements, compounds, and mixtures
- Periodic table: arrangement, groups (alkali metals, halogens, noble gases)
- Chemical bonding: ionic and covalent bonding

**Physics**
- Kinematics: speed, velocity, acceleration, distance-time graphs
- Pressure: in solids, liquids, and gases (hydraulics, atmosphere)
- Thermal physics: conduction, convection, radiation

---

## Grade 9 (Secondary 3 — Pure Sciences begin)

**Biology (Pure)**
- Cell biology: cell division (mitosis), specialised cells
- Biological molecules: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, enzymes
- Photosynthesis: light-dependent and light-independent reactions
- Ecology: ecosystems, food webs, carbon and nitrogen cycles

**Chemistry (Pure)**
- Stoichiometry: formula mass, moles, percentage composition
- Acids, bases, and salts: reactions, preparation, neutralisation
- Oxidation and reduction: redox reactions, electron transfer
- Electrolysis: applications and products

**Physics (Pure)**
- Energy, work, and power
- Waves: properties, reflection, refraction, diffraction
- Electromagnetic spectrum: types, uses, and dangers
- Electricity: current, voltage, resistance, power calculations

---

## Grade 10 (Secondary 4 — O-Level Year)

**Biology**
- Genetics: DNA structure, protein synthesis, Mendelian inheritance
- Evolution: natural selection, selective breeding
- Homeostasis: thermoregulation, osmoregulation, blood glucose control
- Human impact on environment: pollution, conservation

**Chemistry**
- Organic chemistry: alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids
- Macromolecules: plastics, natural and synthetic polymers
- Chemical energetics: exothermic/endothermic reactions, bond energies
- Rate of reaction: factors and collision theory

**Physics**
- Nuclear physics: radioactivity, types of radiation, half-life
- Magnetism and electromagnetism: motors, transformers
- Static electricity: charging by friction, induction, applications
- Space physics: Earth, solar system, stars and universe
