# Australian Science Curriculum — Grades 5 to 10
### Aligned with Australian Curriculum (ACARA)

---

## Grade 5

**Biological Sciences**
- Living things: classification of plants, animals, fungi, bacteria
- Life cycles: reproduction in plants and animals
- Ecosystems: food chains, producers and consumers

**Chemical Sciences**
- Solids, liquids, and gases: properties and changes of state
- Reversible and irreversible changes (e.g., melting, burning)

**Physical Sciences**
- Forces: gravity, friction, push and pull
- Light: reflection, shadows, how we see objects

**Earth & Space Sciences**
- Solar system: planets, sun, moon, stars
- Weather patterns: seasonal changes, rain cycle

---

## Grade 6

**Biological Sciences**
- Cells: basic structure of plant and animal cells
- Body systems: digestive, respiratory, circulatory systems
- Adaptations: how living things survive in their environment

**Chemical Sciences**
- Elements and compounds: introduction
- Mixtures: separating mixtures (filtration, evaporation, sieving)

**Physical Sciences**
- Electricity: circuits, conductors, insulators
- Energy: forms of energy — heat, light, sound, electrical

**Earth & Space Sciences**
- Rocks and soil: types, formation, weathering
- Earth's resources: land, water, air — use and conservation

---

## Grade 7

**Biological Sciences**
- Cell biology: organelles, plant vs animal cells
- Reproduction: sexual and asexual reproduction
- Nutrition: food groups, digestive system in detail

**Chemical Sciences**
- Periodic table: introduction to elements and symbols
- Atoms and molecules: basic atomic structure
- Physical and chemical changes

**Physical Sciences**
- Forces: balanced and unbalanced forces, gravity, magnetism
- Sound: vibrations, pitch, loudness, echoes

**Earth & Space Sciences**
- Earth's structure: crust, mantle, core
- Plate tectonics: earthquakes, volcanoes, continental drift

---

## Grade 8

**Biological Sciences**
- Genetics: DNA basics, inherited and environmental traits
- Evolution: natural selection, adaptation, fossil record
- Ecosystems: biotic and abiotic factors, biodiversity

**Chemical Sciences**
- Atomic structure: protons, neutrons, electrons, Bohr model
- Chemical reactions: reactants, products, word and symbol equations
- Acids and bases: pH scale, neutralisation, everyday examples

**Physical Sciences**
- Motion: speed, distance, time, distance-time graphs
- Heat: conduction, convection, radiation, insulation

**Earth & Space Sciences**
- Climate and weather: greenhouse effect, climate change
- Water cycle: surface water, groundwater, ocean systems

---

## Grade 9

**Biology**
- Cell processes: photosynthesis, respiration, osmosis, diffusion
- Body systems in depth: immune, endocrine, nervous systems
- Ecology: energy pyramids, nutrient cycles, human impact

**Chemistry**
- Periodic table: trends, groups (metals, non-metals, noble gases)
- Chemical bonding: ionic and covalent bonds
- Stoichiometry: balancing equations, mole concept (introduction)
- Acids, bases, and salts: reactions and applications

**Physics**
- Energy: kinetic, potential, conservation of energy
- Electricity: current, voltage, resistance, Ohm's Law
- Waves: transverse and longitudinal, properties of light

---

## Grade 10

**Biology**
- Genetics: Mendelian inheritance, Punnett squares, mutations
- Biotechnology: genetic engineering, cloning, ethics
- Evolution: evidence, mechanisms, species diversity

**Chemistry**
- Reactions: rates of reaction, factors affecting reaction rate
- Electrochemistry: electrolysis, galvanic cells
- Organic chemistry: introduction to carbon compounds and polymers

**Physics**
- Motion and forces: Newton's Laws, momentum, circular motion
- Nuclear physics: radioactivity, half-life, fission and fusion
- Electromagnetic spectrum: uses and applications (X-rays, UV, IR)
