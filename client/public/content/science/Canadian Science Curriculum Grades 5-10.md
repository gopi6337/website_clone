# Canadian Science Curriculum & Syllabus — Grades 5 to 10
### Aligned with Provincial Curriculum Standards (Across Major Provinces)

---

## Grade 5

**Life Science**
- Human body: major organs and their functions
- Biodiversity: classification of living things
- Wetland ecosystems: plants, animals, and interdependence

**Physical Science**
- Forces and simple machines: levers, inclined planes, pulleys
- Properties of matter: mass, volume, density

**Earth & Space Science**
- Weather systems: clouds, wind, precipitation
- Solar system: planets, moons, asteroids

---

## Grade 6

**Life Science**
- Biodiversity: species, ecosystems, food chains
- Flight: birds, insects, and aircraft (lift, drag, thrust)

**Physical Science**
- Air and aerodynamics: properties of air, flight principles
- Electricity: static electricity, circuits, conductors/insulators

**Earth & Space Science**
- Space exploration: planets, satellites, telescopes
- Rocks and minerals: formation, classification, uses

---

## Grade 7

**Life Science**
- Interactions in ecosystems: biotic/abiotic, energy flow
- Plant and animal cells: structure and function
- Reproduction in living things: asexual and sexual

**Physical Science**
- Pure substances and mixtures: solutions, mechanical mixtures
- Heat and temperature: conduction, convection, radiation

**Earth & Space Science**
- Earth's crust: plate tectonics, earthquakes, volcanoes
- Water systems: ocean, freshwater, water cycle

---

## Grade 8

**Life Science**
- Cells, tissues, organs: from cell to body systems
- Excretion and fluid regulation: kidneys, skin
- Pathogens and disease: bacteria, viruses, immune response

**Physical Science**
- Fluids: viscosity, pressure, hydraulic systems
- Mechanical advantage: complex machines

**Earth & Space Science**
- Earth's systems: rock cycle, carbon cycle, nitrogen cycle
- Climate change: evidence, causes, and Canadian context

---

## Grade 9

**Biology**
- Cell biology: cell processes, osmosis, diffusion
- Genetics: DNA structure, inheritance, chromosomes
- Evolution: natural selection, adaptation, fossil evidence
- Ecology: biomes of Canada, food webs, population dynamics

**Chemistry**
- Atomic theory: Bohr model, protons, neutrons, electrons
- Periodic table: families, properties, trends
- Chemical bonding: ionic, covalent, molecular compounds
- Chemical reactions: synthesis, decomposition, combustion

**Physics**
- Motion: speed, velocity, acceleration, distance-time graphs
- Forces: Newton's Laws, friction, gravity
- Work and energy: mechanical energy, energy transformation

---

## Grade 10

**Biology**
- Photosynthesis and cellular respiration: equations and processes
- Nervous system and senses
- Homeostasis: body temperature, blood sugar regulation
- Biotechnology: GMOs, cloning basics, ethical considerations

**Chemistry**
- Stoichiometry: mole concept, balanced equations
- Solutions chemistry: solubility, concentration, acids and bases
- Organic chemistry: hydrocarbons, fossil fuels

**Physics**
- Waves: properties, electromagnetic spectrum, sound
- Electricity: Ohm's Law, series and parallel circuits, power
- Nuclear physics: radioactivity, fission, fusion (introduction)
