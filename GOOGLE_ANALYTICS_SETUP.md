## 🎓 Understanding Your Data

### Key Metrics Explained

**Users:** Unique visitors to your site
**Sessions:** Individual visits (one user can have multiple sessions)
**Engagement Rate:** % of sessions that lasted 10+ seconds, had 1+ conversion, or 2+ page views
**Average Engagement Time:** How long users spend on your site
**Events:** Actions users take (page views, clicks, scrolls, etc.)

### Traffic Sources

**Direct:** Users typed your URL directly or used a bookmark
**Organic Search:** Found you via Google, Bing, etc.
**Referral:** Clicked a link from another website
**Social:** Came from social media platforms
**Email:** Clicked a link from an email
